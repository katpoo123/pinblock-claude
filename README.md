# Pinterest Ad & Product Pin Hider

This is a free browser extension that automatically hides sponsored ads and
shoppable product pins from your Pinterest home feed, so you can scroll in peace.

---

## What you need

- The **Google Chrome** browser
- The folder containing these files (you already have it if you're reading this!)

---

## How to install it (one-time setup)

1. Open Chrome and type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar, then press **Enter**.

2. In the top-right corner of the page, find the toggle labelled **Developer mode**
   and switch it **on**. A few new buttons will appear at the top of the page.

3. Click the **Load unpacked** button.

4. A file picker window will open. Navigate to the folder that contains these files
   (the one with `manifest.json` and `content.js` in it) and click **Select** (Mac)
   or **Select Folder** (Windows).

5. The extension will appear in your list with the name
   **Pinterest Ad & Product Pin Hider**. That's it — you're done!

6. Open (or refresh) Pinterest. Ads and product pins will be hidden automatically.

---

## How to update it after any changes to the files

If you ever edit `content.js` or `manifest.json`, Chrome won't pick up the changes
automatically. Here's how to refresh it:

1. Go back to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Find **Pinterest Ad & Product Pin Hider** in the list.
3. Click the **circular reload arrow** icon on its card.
4. Switch back to your Pinterest tab and press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows)
   or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac) to fully refresh the page.

---

## How to turn it off temporarily

On the `chrome://extensions` page, find the extension and click its toggle to
switch it **off**. Flip it back on any time to re-enable it.

---

## Troubleshooting

**Ads are still showing up.**
Try doing a full page refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac).
Pinterest loads content in stages, so it can take a second or two for the extension
to catch everything.

**The extension disappeared from the list.**
This can happen if you moved or renamed the folder after installing. Just repeat the
install steps above, pointing Chrome at the folder in its new location.

---

*This extension runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect any data,
connect to the internet, or send anything anywhere.*
